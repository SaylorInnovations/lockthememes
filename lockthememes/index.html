When do new tokens drop?

The first launch is scheduled for October 17, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern (EDT / UTC−4). Future launch dates will be announced in our Telegram community. Each published launch appears above earlier launches, which remain available below.

What does “40% locked” mean?

The plan is to place 40% of a token’s total supply into a time-based token lock. The actual amount, supply basis, duration and release terms must be published for each token before its lock can be evaluated.

How long will the tokens be locked?

The team has not announced a lock duration yet. Each launch will display its own start date, unlock date and any staged release schedule once those terms are confirmed.

Is this a liquidity lock?

This commitment concerns token supply. A liquidity lock is separate. Any liquidity lock will need its own clearly labeled record and verification link.

Where can I verify a lock?

Once a lock is created and its details are supplied, the launch record will include a link to the lock provider and the on-chain transaction. There are currently no published lock records on this site.

Does a lock make a token risk-free?

No. A supply lock alone does not establish the safety of a token, its liquidity or the rest of its supply. Review the contract, distribution, lock terms and trading risks independently.
